Georgia Billing & Privacy Notice

Last updated September 5, 2026

This notice supplements, and does not replace, Boasted's Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

It summarizes how Boasted intends to handle recurring subscriptions, customer information, security incidents, account eligibility, and customer requests for users in Georgia. If another Boasted policy gives you greater rights or protections, that policy still applies.

Account eligibility and required agreements

Use Boasted only if you are legally permitted to use the Service under the Terms and applicable law. New email/password registration requires affirmative acceptance of the current Terms and acknowledgment of the current Privacy Policy.

Boasted records the applicable Terms version, Privacy Policy version, and server-side acceptance time on the account. This consent record is maintained separately from the visual state of the registration checkboxes.

Education-related features may be used to document college, university, trade, technical, certification, bootcamp, continuing-education, internship, program, or professional-development evidence, subject to the Terms and applicable law.

Recurring Boasted Pro subscriptions

When Boasted offers a recurring paid subscription, the price, billing interval, automatic-renewal terms, and material subscription terms should be shown before you complete the purchase. Boasted will obtain your consent before charging the payment method used for the subscription.

Unless the checkout says otherwise, a recurring subscription renews for the stated billing period until you cancel it. You may cancel future renewal using the billing controls available in your Boasted account or by contacting Boasted support. Cancellation normally stops future renewal while paid access continues through the period already purchased, subject to the Terms and any rights required by law.

Renewal and billing notices

For online automatic-renewal subscriptions covered by Georgia law, Boasted intends to provide the notices required by applicable law, including information about the renewal period, how to cancel, and how to contact us. If material subscription terms change, we will provide notice as required and include information about cancellation.

If you believe you were charged after canceling, did not authorize a subscription, or cannot find a reasonable way to cancel, contact us promptly so we can investigate.

Payments

Boasted may use a third-party payment processor such as Stripe to collect and process payment information. Boasted should not receive or store your full payment-card number as part of its normal application database. We may receive limited billing identifiers and subscription/payment status needed to operate your account.

Your career and education proof and privacy

Your private Boasted workspace is intended to stay private unless you deliberately use a feature that shares or publishes content. You are responsible for making sure you have permission to store or share employer, client, customer, school, program, or third-party information.

Do not upload passwords, access tokens, API keys, customer secrets, restricted code, protected student records, confidential documents, school-system credentials, or other material you are not allowed to retain. For sensitive work or education records, use a safe summary or approved reference when possible.

Access, correction, export, and deletion requests

Boasted's Privacy Policy describes the account and privacy requests we support. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to identity verification and limited retention that may be necessary for security, billing, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Security incidents

No online service can promise perfect security. Boasted uses administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect customer information. If a security incident creates a legal duty to notify affected Georgia residents, Boasted will provide notice in accordance with applicable Georgia law.

AI-assisted features

Boasted's AI-assisted features are drafting, coaching, and organization tools. AI-generated material can be incomplete or inaccurate. Review generated resumes, interview answers, claims, dates, metrics, academic facts, credentials, and other career or education material before using or sharing it. Boasted does not guarantee a job, interview, promotion, compensation change, employer decision, admission decision, scholarship, or other career or education outcome.

Verification requests

Impact Receipt verification is intended to let a person who knows the work respond to a specific claim. A response is not a background check, audit, legal certification, school certification, or guarantee by Boasted. Do not use verification requests for spam, harassment, marketing, retaliation, or unrelated outreach.

Questions or complaints

For billing, privacy, deletion, account, or security questions, contact Tobias.scott@boasted.io.

Please include enough information for us to identify the account or transaction, but do not email passwords, authentication tokens, full payment-card numbers, or unnecessary confidential evidence.

Boasted is working to keep its customer-facing documentation aligned with current product behavior and applicable law. This supplemental notice may be updated as the product, providers, or legal requirements change.
