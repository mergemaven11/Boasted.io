# BragStack interim age and student-access policy

**Effective:** September 4, 2026  
**Status:** Interim risk-reduction policy pending attorney review.

BragStack accounts are currently limited to people **age 18 or older**. This boundary stays in place until youth-facing privacy, consent, safety, retention, school-data, and related requirements are reviewed with qualified counsel and implemented in the product.

## What is available now

Adults may use BragStack to document:

- current and previous work;
- college or university accomplishments;
- certifications, courses, bootcamps, and professional development;
- internships, projects, research, awards, and other education history;
- prior high-school experience when useful to their present career or education record.

## Middle School — Coming Soon

**Middle School student accounts are not available today.** The feature remains visible in the product roadmap as **Coming soon** so we do not lose the product direction, but it is disabled for new records and must not be marketed as currently available to minors.

Do not invite or instruct a person under 18 to create an account, upload a school record, submit an essay, provide verifier information, or enter other personal information into BragStack.

## Registration control

New email/password registrations must:

1. pass the blocking 18+ confirmation;
2. affirmatively accept the current Terms and Conditions;
3. affirmatively acknowledge the current Privacy Policy.

The backend records the acceptance timestamp and policy version. New Google/GitHub account creation is temporarily paused so OAuth cannot bypass this gate. Existing users who already linked OAuth may continue to sign in.

## Customer-support response for an under-18 request

Use a short, neutral response such as:

> BragStack accounts are currently available only to people age 18 or older. Student accounts for younger users are planned, but they are not open yet while we complete the privacy, consent, and safety work required for that release. Please do not send us school records or other personal information in the meantime.

Do not ask the person for a birth date, school, parent information, educational records, or other personal data merely to explain the restriction.

## Product rules until counsel approves a youth launch

- Keep Middle School visibly labeled **Coming soon** and disabled for new records.
- Do not add an under-18 bypass, invitation code, beta cohort, or parental checkbox without legal review.
- Do not market BragStack as a product for children or younger teens.
- Do not run youth-targeted ads or analytics experiments.
- Keep AI/interview features adult-only under the same account restriction.
- If BragStack discovers an account that appears to belong to a person under 18, stop before collecting more information and escalate internally for a privacy-safe handling decision.
- Do not promise that the present 18+ control is a complete legal solution; it is an interim product boundary.

## Release gate for future under-18 accounts

Before enabling youth/student accounts, require a documented review of at least:

- age screening and age-assurance design;
- parental/guardian notice and consent where applicable;
- parent/guardian access, correction, deletion, and revocation workflows where applicable;
- data minimization and retention/deletion rules;
- school/student-record implications, including whether FERPA, COPPA, state student-privacy laws, or school contracts apply to the intended model;
- AI and conversational-safety requirements for minors;
- advertising, analytics, cookie, and tracking restrictions;
- verifier/contact-data collection involving students;
- vendor agreements and subprocessors that may receive youth data;
- incident-response procedures for youth data;
- updated Terms, Privacy Policy, product copy, support procedures, and accessibility review;
- attorney approval for the jurisdictions in which the youth feature will launch.

## Existing accounts

The 18+ gate applies prospectively to new accounts. Before a broad commercial launch, review legacy accounts created before this control and decide with counsel whether re-attestation or another account review is appropriate.

## Ownership

Until attorney review is complete, the founder is the escalation point for any request involving a suspected under-18 user, parental request, school request, or proposal to enable student accounts.
